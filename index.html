# about

Hands-on leader,
always building.

I'm the person you bring in when there's no codebase yet — and you need one that will still make sense two years from now.

Over 17 years, I've done it four times as co-founder and first engineer: picked the stack, shipped the first lines, hired the first engineers, and built the processes that let teams scale without burning out.

I combine strong technical ownership with product instinct — I care about architecture because I care about shipping. I work AI-native: I don't just build AI features, I use AI at every layer of how I work.

I've taken products from zero to production, navigated a successful acquisition by TotalEnergies, and shipped products used across Europe, Africa, and the Americas — touching Energy, HealthTech, Digital Adoption, and more.

When I'm not doing that, I'm tinkering — side projects, open source, or just building things that scratch my own itch. It's how I stay sharp and ahead of the curve.
